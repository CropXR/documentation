# Concepts

## Metadata

Metadata is the information that describes your data: the plants, growth conditions, treatments, samples and measurements. You enter it in the [catalogue](https://catalogue.cropresilience.org), following the [step by step instructions](../resilience-hub/seek/index.md).

Annotating your data is your responsibility as a researcher; DataXR provides the tools and help. CropXR combines established standards for plant research:

- **ISA** (Investigation-Study-Assay), for structuring research data
- **MIAPPE** (Minimum Information About Plant Phenotyping Experiments), for phenotyping studies
- **ENA** (European Nucleotide Archive), for sequencing data

!!! tip "Plan enough time for metadata"
    The model is flexible enough to fit many types of studies, so you decide how to apply it to yours. Plan enough time the first time you enter metadata. If you are stuck, email the DataXR team at [data@cropxr.org](mailto:data@cropxr.org).

## Research data

Data is stored in the Resilience Hub. Each dataset is linked to an assay in the catalogue, and who can download it follows the sharing settings of your study.

Data is uploaded and downloaded with the [command line tool](../resilience-hub/cli-tool/index.md):

- **Uploading:** enter your metadata in the catalogue first, then upload a folder of data to the matching assay.
- **Downloading:** find the dataset in the catalogue and use its identifier to download it.
